Programme one

Read-a-story

One-to-one English reading sessions over a phone call, linking a volunteer proficient in English with a child who wants to improve. We operate in tribal and underserved parts of India.

Why it matters

  • Builds spoken English confidence through consistent, personal attention
  • Works over a simple phone call — no data connection needed on the child's end
  • Open to volunteers anywhere in the world

Programme two

Solve-with-Bharat

Foundational numeracy practice for tribal students, combining a mobile-based practice app with trained local Community Facilitators, delivered during school hours.

Why it matters

  • Addresses a foundational learning gap affecting rural Maharashtra
  • Creates local employment for Community Facilitators
  • Proven in a concluded quasi-experimental trial with a large effect size
