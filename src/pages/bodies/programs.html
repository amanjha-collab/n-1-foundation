Programme two

Solve-with-Bharat

A practice-based math programme ensuring mastery of basic operations (+, −, ×, ÷) for children in under-resourced Zilla Parishad schools. Weak teacher–student ratios leave gaps in basic math — we bridge them with focused practice and personal support.

Why it matters

  • Custom math practice app and paper-based tools
  • Local facilitators provide personalised attention
  • Rigorous monitoring & evaluation from IIT Bombay
