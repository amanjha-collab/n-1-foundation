Read-a-story

One-to-one English reading sessions, over a phone call

Read-a-story, a n+1 Social Foundation initiative, supported by multiple CSRs, foundations, trusts and individual donors, hopes to improve reading and spoken English in the underserved regions of India. We link a person proficient in English with a child who wishes to improve his or her English. They conduct phone sessions in a 1:1 format. We operate in tribal and underserved parts of India.

How Read-a-story Works

The Concept

Voice, a powerful medium

A child reads aloud from a physical storybook while a volunteer tutor follows along on a digital copy. Through regular phone calls, the tutor guides pronunciation, comprehension and confidence — no app, no video, no data connection required on the child's side.
