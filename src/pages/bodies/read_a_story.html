How Read-a-story Works

One volunteer, one child, one story at a time — over a simple phone call.

The concept

Voice, a powerful medium

A volunteer in the city reads with the child over an ordinary mobile call. Both sides of the line hold the same book — the child a hard copy, the volunteer a digital one. No app, no data plan, no classroom required.
