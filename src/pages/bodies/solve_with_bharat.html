Solve-with-Bharat

Foundational numeracy, powered by technology and community facilitators

Solve-with-Bharat is n+1 Social Foundation's foundational numeracy programme, strengthening children's grasp of addition, subtraction, multiplication and division through a mobile-based practice app, guided by trained local Community Facilitators (CFs) — delivering regular, retrieval-based practice within school hours.

How Solve-with-Bharat Works

The Concept

Practice makes mastery

Children practise level-appropriate maths on a shared mobile phone in small groups, three sessions a week, while a trained local Community Facilitator guides the session and tracks progress — combining affordable technology with the human support that makes it stick.
